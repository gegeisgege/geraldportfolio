Research

Undergraduate Thesis, ITS 2026

Hierarchical Cluster Analysis of Medical Student Performance Across Multiple Assessment Modalities

Gerald Mahapranaja Pillian & Ahmad Muklason, S.Kom., M.Sc., Ph.D.

Applied Ward's linkage hierarchical clustering (Euclidean distance, Z-score standardised) to 98 medical students at FKK ITS across written, practical, standardised-patient, and problem-based assessments. Found three statistically distinct clusters — At-Risk (26.5%), Mixed Performance (9.2%), Average (64.3%) — validated with one-way ANOVA (p < 0.001), Silhouette, Calinski-Harabasz, and Davies-Bouldin indices, intended to support early identification of at-risk students.
