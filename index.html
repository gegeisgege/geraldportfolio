Control Systems 2025

Fuzzy Logic Dual-Axis Targeting System

Hybrid tracker for a two-axis turret. A custom YOLO detector (5,000 augmented images, 95% mAP) feeds a zero-order Sugeno fuzzy controller that turns pixel error and target size into bounded velocity commands, with no plant model. In noisy Monte Carlo runs it converged in 3.0 s against 4.8 s for an inverse-kinematics controller and 16.6 s for PID, and on the physical turret its RMS error was 2.92° against 4.26° for PID. Co-authored with Julián Hernández Alás.

Undergraduate Thesis, ITS 2026

Hierarchical Cluster Analysis of Medical Student Performance Across Multiple Assessment Modalities

Gerald Mahapranaja Pillian & Ahmad Muklason, S.Kom., M.Sc., Ph.D.

Applied Ward's linkage hierarchical clustering (Euclidean distance, Z-score standardised) to 98 medical students at FKK ITS across written, practical, standardised-patient and problem-based assessments. Found three statistically distinct clusters, validated with one-way ANOVA (p < 0.001), Silhouette, Calinski-Harabasz and Davies-Bouldin indices, intended to support early identification of at-risk students.

Understanding User Perceptions of Camera and Microphone Access in Mobile Applications: Exploring Permission Granting and Associated Security and Privacy Risks

Gerald Mahapranaja Pillian, Dimas Gistha Andyana, Muhammad Rafi Novyansyah & Nur Aini Rakhmawati

A mixed-methods study of how well people understand the camera and microphone permissions they grant. A survey of 23 respondents measured knowledge, perceived risk and granting behaviour, while a technical analysis scored Google Play apps by how risky their permission requests look. Users showed little differentiated understanding of what permissions allow, and popular apps were not immune to high risk scores. The paper argues for clearer permission prompts, better privacy education and stricter regulation.

Unpublished manuscript 2025

Fuzzy Logic Control for Dynamic Target Tracking in Two-Axis Autonomous Aiming Systems

Gerald Mahapranaja Pillian & Julián Hernández Alás

Combines a YOLO-based detector with a zero-order Sugeno fuzzy controller (7 × 7 × 5 = 245 rules) that works directly in the image plane and respects the turret's velocity limits. Compared against analytically tuned inverse-kinematics and PID controllers in Monte Carlo simulation with multiplicative noise and on a physical two-axis turret, the fuzzy controller converged faster and with lower steady-state error. The manuscript was not published, but the full text is available to read.
